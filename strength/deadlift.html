Deadlift

Deadlift are a foundational strength training exercise that target multiple muscle groups, enhancing overall fitness and building lower body power.

Types of Deadlift

Bodyweight Deadlift

Perform Deadlift using only your body weight, perfect for beginners or warm-ups.

Barbell Back Deadlift

Use a barbell for added resistance to build strength and muscle mass.

Goblet Deadlift

Hold a dumbbell or kettlebell at your chest for controlled movements.

Bulgarian Split Deadlift

Elevate one foot on a bench to focus on single-leg strength.

Benefits of Deadlift

  • Builds lower body strength and power
  • Enhances core stability and balance
  • Improves mobility and flexibility
  • Boosts athletic performance
  • Supports functional fitness for everyday activities

Muscles Worked During Deadlift

Deadlift are a compound movement that engage the following muscle groups:

  • Quadriceps: The primary movers located at the front of your thighs.
  • Glutes: Targeted for building strength and power in your posterior chain.
  • Hamstrings: Assist in stabilizing the knee joint during Deadlift.
  • Core: Essential for maintaining stability and proper form.
  • Calves: Provide support and balance throughout the movement.

Tips for Effective Deadlift

  • Keep your chest up and back straight during the movement.
  • Engage your core to maintain stability.
  • Lower yourself until your thighs are parallel to the ground or lower.
  • Distribute your weight evenly through your heels.
  • Start with light weights and gradually increase resistance.
